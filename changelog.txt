Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-01-16
  Features:
    - Initial release
    - Added aircraft with take-off and landing mechanics
    - Added three tiers of anti-aircraft worms (small, medium, big)
    - Aircraft can fly over water and obstacles when airborne
    - AA worms spawn naturally in enemy territory
    - AA worms target and shoot down aircraft with homing rockets
  Balance:
    - Aircraft has 800 HP with 40% explosion resistance
    - Aircraft requires 12 engines, 40 steel, 20 circuits, 15 gears
    - Small AA worms deal 75 damage with 40 tile range
    - Medium AA worms deal 125 damage with 50 tile range
    - Big AA worms deal 175 damage with 60 tile range
  Settings:
    - Added setting for takeoff time (1-10 seconds)
    - Added setting for AA worm spawn rate multiplier
    - Added setting to enable/disable flight status messages
